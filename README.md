# 3D Printing Legislation Tracker

A community tracker of laws and bills aimed at 3D printers and 3D-printed firearms — what each one actually says, where it stands, and what it means for the people who print. Maintained by the [3D Printing Weekly meetup](https://maxsikorski.github.io/3d-printing-weekly-news).

**Live site:** https://maxsikorski.github.io/3d-printing-regulation/

- Plain HTML/CSS/JS, no build step. All content lives in `data.js`.
- Follow updates via [RSS](https://maxsikorski.github.io/3d-printing-regulation/feed.xml) or by watching this repo.
- Spotted a bill we missed? Open an issue.

Not legal advice — every card links its primary sources.
